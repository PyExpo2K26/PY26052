PURE CHECK is a portable, handheld device designed to detect milk adulteration instantly at the point of testing while generating court-admissible legal evidence and real-time regulatory insights for FSSAI. The system integrates six sensors—pH, TDS, turbidity, electrical conductivity, density using a load cell, and color—connected to an ESP32 microcontroller along with a GPS module and cloud connectivity. The user simply pours milk into the built-in container and presses a button, after which all sensors collect data within 60 seconds while the GPS captures precise location coordinates and a satellite-based timestamp. A sensor fusion algorithm analyzes these parameters to produce a quality score and identify common adulterants such as water, detergent, starch, and urea, while also flagging potential steroid contamination through multi-parameter anomaly detection for further laboratory confirmation. Each test generates secure digital evidence, including GPS data, an unfakeable timestamp, a digital signature, and a QR code, ensuring a tamper-proof chain of custody suitable for legal proceedings. The data is stored locally on an SD card and simultaneously uploaded to a cloud dashboard, enabling FSSAI authorities to monitor real-time adulteration heatmaps, receive instant alerts, analyze trends, and track field officer performance. Designed to be lightweight, battery-operated, and cost-effective—up to ten times cheaper than existing portable testers—PURE CHECK enables large-scale deployment and shifts milk quality enforcement from delayed laboratory analysis to proactive, on-the-spot action, ultimately helping ensure safer milk for millions of consumers.
